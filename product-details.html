Indoor plants

Snake Plant

GH₵ 25

Snake Plant for indoor spaces.

Care and suitability

Suitable for
Indoor spaces.
Watering
Let the potting mix dry out completely before watering again. Water thoroughly, then let excess drain; water less often in low light or cooler weather.
Sunlight
Bright, indirect light is ideal. Snake Plants tolerate lower light, but avoid prolonged harsh direct sun.
Soil
Use a loose, fast-draining potting mix, such as a cactus and succulent mix.
Care recommendation
Use a pot with drainage holes and do not leave the roots standing in water.

Outdoor plants

Outdoor Plant

GH₵ 15

Add greenery to patios, balconies, and gardens. Outdoor plants have different care needs, so adjust watering and placement to the plant and local conditions.

Care and suitability

Suitable for
Outdoor spaces such as patios, balconies, and gardens. Match the plant to the amount of sun, shade, and shelter available.
Watering
Check the soil regularly and water when the top 2–3 cm feels dry. Water deeply, then allow excess to drain; check more often during hot, dry weather and less during rainy periods.
Sunlight
Needs depend on the plant: sun-loving varieties need several hours of direct sun, while many leafy plants prefer bright indirect light or partial shade.
Soil
Use loose, well-draining soil. For container plants, choose a potting mix and a pot with drainage holes to prevent waterlogged roots.
Care recommendation
Protect plants from conditions they do not tolerate, and adjust watering to rainfall, heat, and how quickly the soil dries.
